# Máster en Inteligencia Artificial Aplicada al Sector Financiero

This section documents the subjects completed and currently studied during my Master's Degree, together with the knowledge and practical skills acquired throughout the program.

## Progress

### ✅ 01 — Inteligencia Artificial: La revolución del dato en finanzas

This subject introduces the role of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in the financial sector. It covers Big Data, the processing of financial data, predictive models and an introduction to generative AI, while examining the opportunities and challenges of applying AI to finance.

**Status:** Completed

### ✅ 02 — Visualización y análisis: Inteligencia de datos para decisiones financieras

This subject focuses on turning data into useful information for financial decision-making. It covers data treatment, connections to internal and external data sources, descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing, analysis of statistical relationships, data preparation for analytical modeling and the creation of effective visualizations.

**Status:** Completed

### 🔄 03 — Gestión avanzada del riesgo crediticio: Algoritmos para transformar la toma de decisiones financieras

This subject applies Machine Learning classification techniques to financial decision-making, with a particular focus on credit risk. It covers classification algorithms and their evaluation, Naive Bayes, K-Nearest Neighbors, decision-tree-based methods such as Bagging and Boosting, and the application of these models to credit-risk assessment.

**Status:** In progress

### 🔄 04 — Inteligencia Artificial estratégica: Modelos y métricas para anticipar el futuro

This subject focuses on advanced techniques and algorithmic tools for evaluating, managing and anticipating credit risk in the financial environment. The contents will be expanded as the subject progresses and the knowledge is consolidated.

**Status:** In progress
